How CatalogAI Works

CatalogAI uses Google Gemini AI to transform raw, incomplete, misspelled industrial product data into commerce-ready, verified records.

Step 01
Smart Product Detection
AI automatically identifies the product type — Motor, Pump, Valve, Sensor, Transformer, Solar Panel, and more — even from messy or incomplete data.
Step 02
Spelling Correction
Misspelled brand names, product types, and specifications are automatically corrected. "siemns" becomes "Siemens", "moter" becomes "Motor".
Step 03
Missing Field Enrichment
For each missing field, AI fills in values using industry knowledge, with a confidence percentage and source citation for every enriched value.
Step 04
Conflict Detection
AI checks for data inconsistencies. If Power = 5W but Voltage x Current = 40,000W, a HIGH severity conflict is flagged.
Step 05
Cross Verification
Mathematical checks are run: Power = V x I x PF, RPM vs frequency and poles, weight validation. Each check returns PASS, FAIL, or CANNOT VERIFY.
Step 06
Human Check Flagging
When AI is not confident (below 60%) or finds multiple possible values, the field is flagged with a clear reason so humans know what to verify.
